Responsible tourism

Ahmedabad's heritage is still lived in. A few simple habits keep it that way for the next visitor — and the families who call it home.

Read the full guidelines
Respect lived-in spaces. Pols and havelis are private homes, ask before entering or photographing.
Support local artisans. Buy crafts, food and guiding services directly from the community.
Leave no trace. Carry your litter out; avoid single-use plastic where possible.
Protect the stonework. Avoid touching carvings and jali screens — natural oils erode soft sandstone.
