Travel that gives back

Responsible tourism

Ahmedabad's old city is still lived in. Visiting responsibly keeps it that way — for residents today and visitors tomorrow.

Guidelines for every visit

Respect religious places. Dress modestly, remove footwear where required, and keep noise low near active worship.
Avoid littering. Carry a reusable bottle and bag; bins are limited inside monuments and stepwells.
Protect historic structures. Avoid climbing on ramparts or leaning on carved surfaces — centuries-old sandstone erodes easily.
Do not damage carvings. Skip touching jali screens and reliefs — natural skin oils accelerate stone decay.
Respect local residents. Pols are private homes — ask before entering courtyards or photographing doorways and residents.
Support local artisans. Buy crafts, textiles and food directly from pol-based makers and market vendors.
Avoid unnecessary crowding. Visit popular sites during quieter hours where possible, especially narrow pol lanes.
Follow photography restrictions. Some shrines and museum galleries restrict photography — check signage and ask staff.
Use sustainable transport. Walk, cycle or use public transport within the old city where feasible.

Connected to global goals

SDG 8 — Decent Work & Economic Growth

Choosing local guides, artisans and food vendors channels tourism spending directly into the community.

SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities & Communities

Protecting carvings, structures and residential lanes keeps the old city's heritage intact for future generations.

SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption & Production

Reducing litter and single-use plastic, and consuming crafts and food responsibly, limits tourism's footprint.
